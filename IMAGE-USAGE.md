# Dewelle image provenance

Images created with the built-in Image Generation tool for this concept:

- `hero.webp`: original collection campaign image.
- `cleanser.webp`, `toner.webp`, `gel.webp`, `cream.webp`, `balm.webp`, `serum.webp`: individual product photography.
- `ritual.webp`: generated adult model lifestyle image, not a real customer or testimonial.
- `texture.webp`: original cream, gel and serum texture composition.

All products and packaging are fictional concept mockups. No existing brand photography or likeness of a named person is used. Optimized WebP versions are stored in the repository. Final prompts are recorded in `IMAGE-PROMPTS.json`.
