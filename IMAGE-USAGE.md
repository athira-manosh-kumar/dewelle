# Dewelle image provenance

Images created with the built-in Image Generation tool for this concept:

- `hero.webp`: original collection campaign image.
- `cleanser.webp`, `toner.webp`, `gel.webp`, `cream.webp`, `balm.webp`, `serum.webp`: individual product photography.
- `ritual.webp`: generated adult model lifestyle image, not a real customer or testimonial.
- `texture.webp`: original cream, gel and serum texture composition.

All products and packaging are fictional concept mockups. No existing brand photography or likeness of a named person is used. Optimized WebP versions are stored in the repository. Final prompts are recorded in `IMAGE-PROMPTS.json`.

- `assets/dew-buddy.webp`: original AI-generated sage jelly dew-drop mascot, transparent background; built-in image generation, 2026-10-11.

## Expanded catalogue photography

The 86 `assets/catalog/*.webp` photos are original AI-generated packaging concepts, produced with the built-in image generation tool. Packaging names, format and size mirror the catalogue data. Text appears only on actual products or their packaging, with props unbranded. SPF, medical efficacy and certification claims are excluded from the image brief. Shade photos show representative packaging, not verified shade swatches.

The nine older hero, product, lifestyle and texture images were visually inspected for branding on props. Their wordmarks are confined to bottles and jars; no background-logo edits were needed. The six older single-product photos are superseded in catalogue cards by the new photography.

The 14 ready combos use one AI-generated group still life per set in `assets/combos/`, rather than tiled individual photos. Prompts list every included product and exclude branding on props. The hero uses CSS camera-like motion and reflection animation over the existing image; it is not video footage.
